1. The five findings that matter

  • Every service query lands on the homepage. In 16 months of Search Console the homepage took 375 of the site's 407 clicks, and every symptom, service and near-me query the site appears for is credited to it. The inner pages' impressions are brand sitelinks: the pediatric page's only real ranking is "pediatric physical therapy" at position 78 to 83 with 3 impressions, the neuro page's is "neuro pt near me" at 9 with 1. Plan v3's reading that neurological and pediatric are "the two service pages Google shows most" is true only because of sitelinks. The rebuild's first job is therefore not to make the service pages better; it is to make them exist in Google's eyes: exact-phrase H1 and title, a Service schema block, and the homepage rows linking to each with the exact service name.
  • The listing wins Chatsworth and nothing else. Local Falcon on 20 Oct 2026: 12 of 13 "service plus chatsworth" terms at 100% share of local voice, pediatric at 97.5%. The open territories are unchanged since April: "physical therapy near me" 27% in the 5-mile grid (the clinic already leads a fragmented pack), Porter Ranch 4.9%, Northridge 0%, West Hills 0%, neurological rehabilitation 31%. The grid holders in Northridge and Porter Ranch are two Northridge listings rated 3.8 and 3.3, which is the opening; the near-me tier above the clinic holds 56 to 137 reviews against 28, which is the ceiling.
  • The name reads as "therapist". 1,240 impressions and 3 clicks in 16 months on "psychotherapy chatsworth", "chatsworth therapist", "therapists chatsworth ca" and "vision loss chatsworth". Every title on the new site says Physical Therapy in full, never "therapist" or "PT" alone, and the FAQ page carries an answer on what the clinic does not treat so those visitors leave on purpose rather than bounce.
  • Northridge is the city page worth building first, and Granada Hills and West Hills are not worth building at all. "physical therapy northridge" is the one neighbouring-city term with demand (94 GSC impressions in 16 months, Semrush 140 a month) and a beatable organic tail below prnpt.com's location page. Porter Ranch has almost no demand (10 impressions in 16 months, Semrush 30) but no clinic of its own; it goes second. Granada Hills and West Hills are held by five and four resident clinics respectively; a Chatsworth clinic's page there fails the real-page test and plan v3's phase 3 list becomes a default no.
  • The conditions layer is uncontested and the clinic already has the proof for most of it. None of the five named competitors ranks for a single condition term; the homepage already surfaces for "knee pain relief" (5.1), "shoulder pain treatment near me" (1.0), "physical therapy for sciatica" (4.0), "physical therapy for plantar fasciitis" (3.0) and "bppv treatment near me" (1.0) with nothing to land on. Twelve conditions pages are specified (plan v3's nine plus frozen shoulder, herniated disc and vertigo/BPPV), each with a named review where one exists and an honest "none yet" where it does not.

The single biggest lever: service and conditions pages that exist as pages in Google's index with the GBP services described to match them. The single biggest blocker: six pages have no matching review and two city pages have no local proof yet; both are Dr. Ed's to supply (section 14).
