Common questions

Do I need a referral from my doctor?

No. California law lets you see a physical therapist without a referral for up to 12 visits or 45 days, whichever comes first. After that, we need a referral from your doctor to continue. Medicare patients need a referral from the start.

Do you take insurance?

We accept Medicare and personal injury liens. If you have PPO insurance, we provide a superbill, an itemized receipt you send to your insurer for reimbursement. Direct-pay patients are welcome. Call (818) 749-3205 and we will walk you through the cost before your first visit.

How long is each visit, and how many will I need?

Visits run about 60 minutes, depending on what you need, and every one is one-on-one with Dr. Ed. How many you need depends on your condition and how your body responds, so Dr. Ed sets the plan after your first evaluation rather than guessing up front.

Will I see Dr. Ed every time?

Yes. Every visit is one-on-one with Dr. Ed, from the first evaluation through discharge.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move in. Shorts for knee, hip or ankle issues, and a tank top or loose shirt for shoulder, neck and back.

Can you treat kids and teen athletes?

Yes. School-age children and teenagers are welcome, including gymnasts and dancers, with a parent in the room for every visit.

Do you treat vertigo, dizziness or stroke recovery?

Yes. Dr. Ed treats vertigo and balance problems, and works with patients recovering from a stroke on strength, walking and everyday movement.

Do you speak Russian?

Yes. Dr. Ed speaks English and Russian, so you can be treated in either.
