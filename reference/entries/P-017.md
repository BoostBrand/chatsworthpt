## FAQs (in the patient's words)
How long is the first visit? About 60 minutes, depending on what you need.
What should I wear? Comfortable clothes; shorts for knee, hip or ankle; a tank top or loose shirt for shoulder, neck and back.
What should I bring? Referral if Medicare, imaging reports, surgeon's protocol, a list of medications.
Will I be treated on the first visit? Yes, the evaluation ends with treatment and a home program.
Can someone come with me? Yes; a parent stays for the whole visit with a child or teen.
