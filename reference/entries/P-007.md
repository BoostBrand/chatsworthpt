## FAQs (in the patient's words)

Can physical therapy help arthritis? Yes: strength, mobility and load management reduce pain and keep the joint moving.
How soon after an injury should I come in? Within the first few days once you can bear weight; fractures and unstable joints get imaged first.
Do I need a referral? Only for Medicare; direct-pay patients book straight away.
Will I see Dr. Ed every time? Yes.
