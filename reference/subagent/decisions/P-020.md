# P-020 Contact and booking, /contact/

Files: src/content/contact.ts and src/pages/contact.astro (InfoPage).

1. Hero: the template builds the contact hero by itself (H1, the phone number first with "Dr. Ed answers his own
   phone.", your lede, the address and hours, and the booking form beside it with the #book anchor). Give it the exact
   H1 and a one-line `lede` (for example: book online or call, and Dr. Ed confirms your time, usually the same day).
   No image. Set `booking: false` (the form is in the hero).
2. Address, hours and parking: kind 'visit', h2 for example "Address, hours and parking". The template prints the
   approved facts table and the map with Get directions and Call. Your `blocks`: one paragraph for parking (free lot in
   front, no gate code, street parking also available; between Winnetka and Corbin, just north of Nordhoff St) and one
   sentence that Dr. Ed treats patients in English or Russian. (This covers the entry's items 2 and 3.)
3. Getting here from each city: kind 'prose', h2 for example "Getting here". One line per city from the entry's list,
   route only, no drive times, then the entry's marker exactly, once, at the end of the section:
   [CONFIRM or verify each: Porter Ranch via Rinaldi and Winnetka; Northridge via Nordhoff; West Hills and Canoga Park via Topanga Canyon or De Soto; Granada Hills via the 118 to Winnetka; Simi Valley via the 118 to Topanga Canyon]
   Chatsworth itself needs no route line. Link the city names Northridge (/physical-therapy-northridge/) and Porter
   Ranch (/physical-therapy-porter-ranch/) with those anchors: while the city pages are switched off the links print as
   plain text by themselves.
4. What happens after you book: kind 'prose' (confirmation usually the same day, what to bring, a link to /first-visit/
   with the anchor "Your first visit", and a link to /insurance-and-cost/ with the anchor "Insurance and cost").
5. Review: kind 'review', key 'mike', context a short label from his review (for example "shoulder pain").
6. Also on: kind 'social', h2 for example "Also on", label 'Follow', text: one sentence with a link to the Google
   listing: [Chatsworth Physical Therapy on Google](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Chatsworth+Physical+Therapy&query_place_id=ChIJjzo2DhibwoAR1KeHYSgrQkE).
   No Yelp link (not confirmed as the clinic's own listing).
7. related: All services (/our-services/), Your first visit (/first-visit/), Insurance and cost (/insurance-and-cost/).
8. Word target 350 to 500.
